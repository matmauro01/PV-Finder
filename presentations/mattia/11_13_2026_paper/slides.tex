\begin{frame}{Before the GNN: Efficiency, Fakes, Splits}
\mtab{%
Efficiency (Eq.~1) & truth vertices ($n_\mathrm{trk}\ge2$) with $\ge1$ reco vertex within \winMM{} mm, over all truth vertices; ratio of sums over events. Equals clean + merged truth of the closest-first matcher (merged credit), event by event. & \texttt{compare\_res\_reco} in \texttt{efficiency\_res\_\allowbreak optimized\_atlas.py}; identity test \texttt{tests/\allowbreak test\_amvf\_fair\_\allowbreak matching.py} \\
One-to-one efficiency & truth vertices that win a dedicated reco vertex in the closest-first assignment & same \\
Fake fraction & reco vertices with no truth vertex within \winMM{} mm, over all reco vertices & same (\texttt{fake}) \\
Fakes per event & the same count over the number of events & same \\
Split & reco vertex with a truth vertex in its window that a closer reco vertex already took & same (\texttt{split}) \\
HS reconstruction & events with a reco vertex within \hsWinMM{} mm of the true HS ($z$ only, ITk Sec.~5.5), over all events & \texttt{gnn/\allowbreak evaluation/\allowbreak hs\_metrics.py} \\
}
\par\vspace{.4em}{\footnotesize Truth: \texttt{TruthVertex} with $\ge2$ selected tracks. AMVF: stored vertices with $n_\mathrm{trk}\ge2$. Errors: event bootstrap (2,000 replicas); PV-Finder minus AMVF is paired on the same resampled events.}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Before the GNN: Resolution and the Window}
\mtab{%
$\svtx$ & all within-event pairs of reco vertices; $\Delta z$ in \pairBins{} bins over $\pm6$ mm; fit $a/(1+e^{b(r-|x|)})+c$; $\svtx=|r|$. Same peaks as the counts. Quoted at PU200, and per $\mu$ bin only for $\mu\ge100$. & \texttt{fit\_dz} in \texttt{run\_eval\_\allowbreak pvf\_run3.py}; \texttt{amvf\_fair\_\allowbreak comparison/\allowbreak scan.py} \\
68\% residual & 68th percentile of $|z_\mathrm{reco}-z_\mathrm{true}|$ after the optimal one-to-one assignment within \assignMM{} mm (most matches, then least total distance). The resolution versus $\mu$. & \texttt{matched\_residuals\_\allowbreak optimal}, \texttt{amvf\_fair\_\allowbreak comparison/\allowbreak matching.py} \\
Window & $3\times$ the larger 68\% residual of the two algorithms at PU200, rounded: $3\times\pseAMVF~\mu$m $=0.1694$ mm $\to$ \winMM{} mm & \texttt{working\_points.\allowbreak MATCH\_WINDOW\_MM} \\
Hard-scatter window & \hsWinMM{} mm, ITk performance paper & \texttt{working\_points.\allowbreak HS\_MATCH\_\allowbreak WINDOW\_MM} \\
}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{After the GNN: Vertex Categories}
\mtab{%
Assignment & each track keeps its top-1 GNN edge if the score is $\ge t=\hlT$; candidates with no track are dropped from the output & \texttt{gnn/evaluation/\allowbreak chain\_inference.py} \\
Track lists & PV-Finder: GNN-assigned tracks. AMVF: its TTVA links (\texttt{Nonprompt\_Hard\_MaxWeight}, \texttt{RecoVertex\_assocTracks}). One rule for both. & \\
Clean, Merged, Fake & per vertex, the plurality truth vertex of its tracks. Fake if the plurality is unlabelled tracks; Clean if its share is $\ge\purityCut$; Merged otherwise & \texttt{classify\_\allowbreak assignments}, \texttt{gnn/evaluation/\allowbreak classification.py} \\
Split & among vertices with the same plurality truth vertex, all but the largest $\sum p_\mathrm{T}^2$ & same \\
Per event & Clean, Merged, Split, Fake vertices per event (bar plot at PU200, versus $\mu$) & \texttt{hs\_metrics.\allowbreak classify\_vertices} \\
Clean/truth & Clean vertices over truth vertices with $n_\mathrm{trk}\ge2$ & \texttt{chain\_t\_summary} \\
Cross-checks & AMVF with its fit tracks and fit weights; PV-Finder with GNN scores as weights & \texttt{weighted\_\allowbreak classification.py} \\
Bounds & finder cap: peaks matched one to one within \winMM{} mm. Truth-guided: each labelled track to the peak nearest its own truth vertex & \texttt{hs\_metrics}, \texttt{chain\_gap\_\allowbreak decomposition.py} \\
}
\par\vspace{.3em}{\footnotesize Errors: event bootstrap (200 replicas), paired. Every category count is cross-checked by independent code.}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{After the GNN: Hard Scatter}
\mtab{%
HS selection & score $=\sum p_\mathrm{T}^2$ over a vertex's tracks with compatibility $\chi^2\le\chiCut$ (Athena 24.0 \texttt{SumPt2Sorting}); selected vertex = argmax. Reconstructed and selected: the selected vertex is the HS match (most HS tracks) and lies within \hsWinMM{} mm of the true HS. AMVF reference: its stored primary. & \texttt{gnn/evaluation/\allowbreak pv\_selection.py}; \texttt{hs\_selection\_\allowbreak identical\_rule.py} \\
HS resolution & events where both algorithms have a vertex within \hsWinMM{} mm of the true HS; $z_\mathrm{reco}-z_\mathrm{HS}$ of the closest; width = iterative RMS within $\pm3$ RMS until stable (Athena \texttt{iterRMS\_convergence}). PV-Finder position: 3D adaptive fit with beam-spot constraint of the GNN-assigned tracks. & \texttt{itk\_resolution.\allowbreak iterative\_rms}; \texttt{hs\_position\_\allowbreak estimators.\allowbreak adaptive\_\allowbreak beamline\_fit} \\
Clean-HS fraction & events with a Clean vertex whose plurality truth is the HS & \texttt{hs\_metrics} \\
HS-track fraction & HS tracks on the HS-matched vertex over all HS tracks & same \\
Local density & truth vertices within $\pm2$ mm of the true HS, HS excluded, over 4 mm (all truth vertices, ITk definition) & \texttt{itk\_resolution.\allowbreak local\_pu\_density} \\
}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{After the GNN: Track Association}
\mtab{%
Correct track & assigned to a vertex whose plurality truth vertex is the track's own & \texttt{chain\_t\_metrics} \\
Track efficiency & correct tracks / truth-labelled tracks; unassigned tracks stay in the denominator & same \\
Track purity & correct tracks / assigned tracks with a truth label & same \\
Track $F_1$ & $2\epsilon p/(\epsilon+p)$ & same \\
Wrong rate & $1-$ purity & same \\
Unassigned fraction & $1-$ (assigned labelled tracks / labelled tracks); efficiency $=$ purity $\times(1-$ unassigned fraction$)$ & \texttt{hllhc\_final\_\allowbreak chain\_extras.py} \\
}
\par\vspace{.4em}{\footnotesize Tracks without a truth label enter neither efficiency nor purity, only the assigned fraction.}
\end{frame}
